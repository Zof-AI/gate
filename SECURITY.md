# Security policy

This client handles a CI secret (`ZOF_GATE_TOKEN`), so we take reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **support@zofai.com** with "Security: Zof-AI/gate" in the subject. Please include the
version or commit, the steps to reproduce, and the impact you see. Do not open a public issue
for a vulnerability, and do not include a real token in your report.

We acknowledge reports within three business days and keep you updated until a fix ships.

## Supported versions

Pre-release: only the latest commit on `main` is supported.
